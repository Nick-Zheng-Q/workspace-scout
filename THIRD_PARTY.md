# Third-party sources and data

- Demo user briefs are hypothetical; their named providers and limited published facts are real, dated research examples, not customer testimonials or current availability guarantees.
- External factual notes use short paraphrases with direct source links. Provider material, names and trademarks remain with their respective owners; the project license does not relicense those materials. No provider endorsement is implied.
- Archived evaluation pages and answers are research records, not fresh quotes. Synthetic fixtures explicitly say they are simulated. Do not market them as real properties or policies.
- Follow host permissions and source terms. Do not bypass logins, CAPTCHAs, anti-bot controls or rate limits. Prefer official operator/application pages and use permitted alternatives when access fails.
- Search, document-processing and installation tools are supplied by the user/host, under their own terms. The repository does not bundle their dependencies or grant access to their accounts.
