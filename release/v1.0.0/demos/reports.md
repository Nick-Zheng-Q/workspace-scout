# Workspace research examples — 19 October 2026

Illustrative briefs using real published sources; not actual customers, bookings, all-market searches or proof of effectiveness. Four-state judgments refer to the exact stated plan, not every service in a building. Current stock, operator approval and complete compulsory costs remain unconfirmed.

## 1. Montréal: is the lowest monthly price the whole commitment?

**Brief:** one consultant, six desk weekdays and four separate one-hour private calls; CAD 350 monthly cap, Plateau or downtown.

**Worth asking ECTO first, not yet a complete fit.** At 936 avenue du Mont-Royal Est, compare its flexible monthly plan with individual day passes plus room hire. The [French tariff page](https://ecto.coop/tous-les-tarifs/) publishes CAD 200 for the flexible package and four monthly meeting-room hours; individual desk days are CAD 30 and weekday room hire CAD 40/hour. All are before tax. Six day passes plus four separate room hours give **CAD 340 before tax**, not a confirmed CAD 340 total. A continuous four-hour room block is not interchangeable with four separate bookings. The same page invites enquiries about tailored arrangements or service exchange; no price or entitlement is promised.

| Hard condition | State / reason |
|---|---|
| Six desk weekdays | Meets the published usage scope; dates and capacity unknown |
| Four confidential calls | Unknown: room-hour credits do not prove availability, acoustic isolation or permission for calls |
| Complete monthly cost ≤ CAD 350 | Unknown: tax and any compulsory extras not confirmed |

The [membership explanation](https://ecto.coop/wp-content/uploads/Tout-sur-vos-frais-dadhesion.pdf) describes CAD 350 in cooperative shares, split CAD 100 / CAD 250, with different repayment restrictions and possible deferral. This is **not ordinary nonrefundable setup or a freely refundable rental deposit**. Confirm whether membership is mandatory for this exact package and when it must be paid. No tax benefit is deducted from the budget; this report makes no tax-eligibility conclusion. The helper intentionally does not misclassify this capital contribution into its limited cost categories.

**Next questions:** Are the four hours separate bookable private calls? What is the all-in quote? Are shares required for this plan and what written repayment terms apply? Request a tailored quote only if the published plan still looks useful. Use the operator's [contact page](https://ecto.coop/contact/); no enquiry was sent.

**Coverage:** direct operator and its linked tariff/membership material, not a fresh citywide shortlist. ## 2. London: free shared desks, not a free private office

**Brief:** two early-stage Camden-based design/software founders, three weekdays weekly, GBP 600 combined monthly cap, occasional client calls, willing to contribute community time.

**Contact-worthy:** Camden Collective free-hotdesking scheme, requesting **Collective Auction Rooms, 5–7 Buck Street, NW1 8NJ**. The [current scheme page](https://camdencollective.co.uk/hotdesking) covers Auction Rooms and Collective School, says applications are being accepted, and describes shared desks and member facilities. **Specific hub assignment and two places are unknown**; do not silently merge the buildings. Admission is case-by-case, focused on early-stage creative/tech work, with two community hours monthly and quarterly reviews. Published meeting facilities do not guarantee a private call slot or acoustic performance. Use the page's application link or its published contact, without assuming acceptance.

An [older/differently titled page](https://camdencollective.co.uk/base-kings-cross) gives narrower trading-stage criteria. **Scope/applicability conflict:** ask which rules govern the present intake rather than applying the strictest line as if it were definitely current. Free hotdesking and paid/subsidised lockable offices are distinct plans.

**Secondary lead needing intake confirmation:** LABS Atrium, Camden Town, six-month council-supported shared membership. The [council offer](https://www.camden.gov.uk/free-co-working-space-for-your-businesses) describes limited places for Camden-based businesses, individual non-shareable memberships and usage review. Its deadline says “7 August” without a year. At this observation date, that does **not** establish a current open round. Do not call it available or apply an award to the budget.

## 3. Singapore: eight day passes or a ten-day monthly plan?

**Brief:** one freelance designer, eight weekdays 9am–5pm around Lavender, SGD 300 cap including GST, occasional private calls; photography use can be separately quoted.

**Two separate contact-worthy plans at the Hive Lavender**, Level 6, 1 Kallang Junction: [operator's membership page](https://thehivelavender.com/membership/) lists day passes at SGD 30 (8am–6pm) and part-time membership at SGD 275 for ten days with the same stated access hours, **exclusive of GST**. Using the [current 9% GST rate](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/goods-services-tax-(gst)/basics-of-gst/current-gst-rates) as the billing scenario:

- Eight day passes: 8 × 30 × 1.09 = **SGD 261.60**, before any unconfirmed mandatory extras.
- Part-time monthly: 275 × 1.09 = **SGD 299.75**, before any unconfirmed mandatory extras.

Published hours meet the brief, not confirmed capacity on eight dates. Private-call suitability, reservation rules, setup/deposit, cancellation and any extra service charges are **unknown**, so neither is an all-in confirmed fit. Building-level phone booths/studios do not mean unlimited exclusive use is included. Ask for specific call-room and studio prices/conditions. The operator advertises limited early-stage special rates; it does not publish an awarded discount for this user. No discount or government grant is deducted. No age or company eligibility is invented.

**Next:** ask the [operator](https://thehivelavender.com/apply/) for date availability, complete GST-inclusive charges, call privacy and studio add-ons; compare flexibility versus a monthly commitment. No account or booking was created. Coverage is a same-operator plan comparison, not a complete Singapore survey.
