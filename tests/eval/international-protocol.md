# International discovery regression protocol

## Purpose

Test whether an agent adapts discovery to the location, follows leads to specific plans and attributable entry points, and reports access limits honestly. Do not reward a named operator, a prescribed website, a candidate count, length, or matching headings. The cases below are reusable prompts. The Montréal pair has been [executed once](results-overseas-ab-2026-10-19.md); the Osaka and mainland-China cases remain **unrun**, not executed test results.

For a comparison, give fresh, isolated runs the same prompt, model/tools, search/time budget and date: one without Workspace Scout, one with it. Do not include this rubric, the source-access report, or example providers in execution context. Preserve actual query/tool records. Randomize the case order; repeat before claiming improvement. This document does not authorize launching extra agents or external actions.

## Prompts

### A — Montréal, mixed-language discovery

I am a solo software consultant comparing Plateau-Mont-Royal and downtown Montréal. I need a desk six weekdays per month and a private room for four one-hour client calls; no storage or address service. Budget CAD 350 per month including mandatory charges. I have a Canadian company and no university affiliation. Find a few specific plans worth contacting, including any credible low-cost alternatives. Please answer in English. Do not contact anyone or submit anything.

### B — Osaka, private room and program eligibility

大阪市内で、デザイン相談業を始める2人用の仕事場を探しています。週3日利用し、顧客との秘密の打合せがあるので個室が必要です。機材は置きません。日本法人はまだなく、大学との関係もありません。月額の上限は必要経費込みで8万円です。民間のオフィスと、応募して利用できる創業支援の場所を比較してください。申込みや連絡はしないでください。

### C — mainland China, incomplete material

两人在广州天河或海珠做内容制作，每周去四天，需要录音的独立房间和设备过夜留置。已有中国公司，月度必要费用上限3000元。请主动寻找商业及可申请的低价空间；不要假设开放工位可录音，也不要只给园区名。不要联系运营方或提交申请。

For a blocked-source variant, use the same prompt and an environment with one normally relevant source unavailable. Do not ask an agent to bypass access controls. A fabricated failure must be labeled as an injected test condition, not a live outage.

## Evidence and review

- **Discovery:** actual locally appropriate queries and source paths, including non-English sources where useful; no mandatory social platform or domain-suffix rule. Absence of a local-language query is not automatically a failure if the authoritative sources already cover the task.
- **Coverage:** commercial and relevant application-based paths explored, or a clear user-scope reason not to; affiliation requirements respected without collecting unneeded identity documents. Alternative forms must address the work, not pad the list.
- **Attribution:** retained options identify an address and a concrete plan; operator-authorized booking/form links can qualify as attributable entry points. A public directory is a lead, not proof of current seats.
- **Access:** distinguish read text, index-only information, official link provenance and unread form content. Do not claim a submitted/tested form, closed program or nonexistent supply from a fetch failure.
- **Fit and cost:** private calls/recording are not inferred from a shared desk; currency, unit, necessary taxes/fees and conditions are explicit. No grant or fitting-out concession becomes unconditional zero-cost office use.
- **Action:** contact or view only with separate authorization. Deliver concrete next questions and separate unmet, unknown and unsearched items.

Independently open the decisive links before grading. Record the user's scope, what was observable, access differences and evidence limits. If both runs cannot access a key source, do not credit the Skill for knowing its contents. Structural/unit tests do not establish better discovery performance.
