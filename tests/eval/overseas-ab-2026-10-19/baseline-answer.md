# Montréal workspace shortlist
*Public-source check: 19 October 2026. No one was contacted.*

**Start with ECTO on the Plateau.** Its published flex membership covers your six weekdays **and four meeting-room hours** within budget. Downtown, AntiCafé is a credible inexpensive desk option, but its private-room price needs clarification.

All figures below are CAD. I conservatively add **5% GST + 9.975% QST**, even where a page does not specify tax treatment. Your $350 cash budget therefore permits about **$304.41 before tax**, including compulsory extras. I have not deducted possible business tax recoveries. [Revenu Québec](https://www-prd.revenuquebec.ca/en/businesses/consumption-taxes/gsthst-and-qst/basic-rules-for-applying-the-gsthst-and-qst/types-of-supplies/taxable-supplies/)

## Lower-cost alternatives and exclusions

- **Municipal libraries:** the city confirms free individual/group study rooms reservable with a library card. Potentially useful for desk days, but your Canadian company does not establish personal library-card eligibility. Solo commercial video calls, acoustic privacy and recurring bookings need checking. I would **not count these as guaranteed client-call rooms**. If eligible library desk access works for you, four paid ECTO room hours would cost approximately **$183.96 including tax**, before any extras. [City library-room rules](https://montreal.ca/en/topics/library-study-rooms)
- **Do not rely on Nuage B’s $150 offer:** the official promotion expired **20 March 2025**, and the main site identifies Saint-Henri rather than either comparison area. [Expired offer](https://nuage-b.com/en/lp-flexible-affordable-hot-desk-space-in-montreal/)
- I have not assumed access to university-only rooms or startup-program space: no university affiliation or qualifying startup status was established.

**What to request when you choose to enquire:** a written total for six full weekdays and four separately reservable, enclosed, acoustically private one-hour calls, including taxes, joining/co-op fees, deposits and any other mandatory charges; plus minimum term, notice period and booking limits. Exclude storage and address-service add-ons.

**Bottom line:** ECTO’s shorter-commitment flex option is the most straightforward first contact. Choose downtown AntiCafé for cheaper desk use only if its own room quote works—or if travelling to the Plateau for calls is acceptable.
