### Fit classification

- **Fully established fits: zero in either answer.** Actual room availability, confidential speech privacy and complete compulsory charges are not established.

- **Additional conditional offers:** A's Anticafé plus ECTO combination has published component prices; B's Anticafé same-site illustration depends on unresolved room-fee and solo-use terms.

## Decisive independent checks

1. **ECTO plan claims are supported in both answers.** The operator confirms $200 monthly, six months, a 20% shorter-commitment surcharge, weekday floating desks and four monthly meeting hours. Its room page offers three rooms to members and visitors, with weekday room hire at $40/hour. Those pages do not establish speech isolation or four separate credit-booking rules. [Plans](https://ecto.coop/nos-bureaux/), [rooms](https://ecto.coop/location-de-salles/).

2. **B catches a material published charge A does not quantify.** ECTO lists $350 lifetime membership shares ($100 ordinary plus $250 preferred). The read page does not settle obligation, timing or refundability. A's generic question about joining/co-op charges is sensible, but reporting the amount materially improves the enquiry and prevents treating a recurring-price calculation as first-payment assurance. This is **not** proof that ECTO necessarily exceeds the monthly budget. The same tariff page also invites a personalized plan or service exchange for constrained budgets, a concrete negotiation route neither answer develops. [ECTO tariffs](https://ecto.coop/tous-les-tarifs/).

3. **District 3 is a genuine but limited addition in B.** Official pages confirm downtown workstations and bookable conference rooms; free startup services; no required university affiliation; Quebec-resident and selection conditions; and the stated program workload/team restrictions. They do not establish free standalone access or four call hours for this consultant. B makes that distinction, although calling it the most important low-cost option is more enthusiastic than the demonstrated personal fit supports. General email is verified. [Space](https://www.district3.co/about), [services](https://www.district3.co/), [programs](https://www.district3.co/programs), [contact](https://www.district3.co/contact).

4. **Anticafé does not decide the comparison by price alone.** Direct pages returned no readable body in this review. Official-domain indexed results support the $21.85 daily cap and $10 exclusive-group-room reservation fee, but not solo eligibility, duration, final charges or acoustic privacy. A sensibly avoids assuming $40 buys the four call rooms; B's cheaper illustration is clearly contingent. I cannot infer that A had the same fetch access during its run. [Rates](https://anticafe.team/), [reservation](https://anticafe.team/reservation).

5. **Comparable correct screening:** both correctly compute CoWork's $431.16 desk-plus-four-paid-hours scenario from indexed operator starting prices; neither rules out included credits. Both treat municipal rooms as an eligibility/privacy lead rather than guaranteed commercial call space. A correctly notices Nuage B's March 2025 promotional expiry. [CoWork](https://montrealcowork.com/), [city rooms](https://montreal.ca/sujets/salles-de-travail-en-bibliotheque), [Nuage B promotion](https://nuage-b.com/en/lp-flexible-affordable-hot-desk-space-in-montreal/).

**Arithmetic:** all quoted monetary calculations checked correctly to cents. Both use the correct 5% GST plus 9.975% QST multiplier; whether an uncertain price already includes tax remains a quote question. [Revenu Québec](https://www.revenuquebec.ca/fr/entreprises/taxes/tpstvh-et-tvq/perception-de-la-tps-et-de-la-tvq/calcul-des-taxes/).

## Limitations

Only the four assigned input files were read; no original query/execution logs were supplied. Therefore I compared coverage demonstrated by the answers and sources, not self-reported French/English searches or process compliance. Review used **3 search queries, 13 opens, 0 clicks and 4 finds**, below the ceilings. It was not an exhaustive supply search. No providers were contacted, forms submitted or purchases made. Failed page retrieval is an evidence limitation, not proof of fabrication. This judgment is local to these two answers and this request; it does not identify their production conditions or establish general method efficacy.
