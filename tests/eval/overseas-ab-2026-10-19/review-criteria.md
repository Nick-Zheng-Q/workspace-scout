# Review criteria fixed before results

Task: one Montréal case, same request/model settings/tools and equal ceilings of 12 search queries and 16 opens/clicks; same-day live web. The explicit-use Skill treatment is the only intended difference. The baseline must not read the Skill or prior evaluations. A single pair can show observable local differences, not general efficacy or which individual instruction caused them.

Compare outcomes, not length, headings, raw candidate count, or number of local-language searches. Check:
1. Concrete space + plan + attributable contact/booking entrance, separated by target area. Count fully established fits separately from plausible contact-worthy plans and incomplete leads; do not reward named brands without offers.
2. Search coverage observable in query logs: relevant commercial and credible low-cost/application channels, appropriate local terminology when useful, no assumed university affiliation. Missing a query language alone is not a failure if authoritative coverage is adequate.
3. Correct linkage of each claim to the location/plan, distinguishing operator pages, authorized booking services, general directory leads and unread index snippets. Readable current source > unverified claim of terms; an inaccessible page must not be treated as nonexistent supply.
4. Practical fit for six desk days and four private hour-long calls. Shared desk, general meeting facility or a phone booth is not automatically an included/bookable private room; decisive unknowns must be explicit.
5. Cost: CAD, expected quantities, necessary tax/fees, policy conditions, no unverified free or subsidized claim. Over-budget lower bounds must not be recommended as confirmed within budget.
6. Useful next contact actions and questions, no invented entrance, no external mutation.

Verify decisive source claims independently. A source that differs in accessibility between execution and review is an evidence limitation, not automatic fabrication. Record serious errors and comparable strengths separately. Do not infer superiority from cautious wording alone. A neutral reviewer will receive randomized answer labels without condition mapping; execution log compliance will be audited separately.
