## Downtown: District 3 community coworking
**1250 Guy Street, suite 600.** Its current official page describes first-come-first-served workstations, weekdays 9 a.m.–5 p.m., and bookable rooms for hybrid meetings. Its startup services are free and take no equity. This is the most important low-cost option to investigate—not an established $0 rental entitlement. [Workspace description](https://www.district3.co/about), [free services](https://www.district3.co/).

**Worth a targeted enquiry:** Is there free standalone community coworking for a solo incorporated software consultant, or must you enter a program? What room quota, charges and booking rights would you receive?

**Eligibility status:** no university affiliation required—**met** for programs. Programs are open to Quebec residents; your Canadian company does not establish your personal Quebec residency, so that condition is **unknown**. Acceptance and access without program participation are also **unknown**. The website says program applications are open, but admission takes a panel decision, typically 2–3 weeks. [Program eligibility and application entry](https://www.district3.co/programs).

Do not join an intensive merely for a cheap desk: Validation requires approximately 25 hours weekly plus a three-hour weekly in-person session. Launch & Grow requires at least two co-founders, so that route **does not meet** your stated solo situation. Four private call hours, sound isolation and the complete workspace cost remain **unknown**. Contact **info@district3.co** through the verified [official contact page](https://www.district3.co/contact).
